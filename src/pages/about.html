About and privacy

What this site is, and what happens to what you type.

What this is

{{siteName}} is a collection of small, single-purpose web tools. Each one does a single job, opens fast, and gives you a result you can copy, download or print right away.

Your data stays on your device

Every tool runs entirely in your web browser. When you type text, open a photo, paste JSON or generate a password, nothing is sent to a server. There are no accounts, no tracking scripts and no advertising cookies. Random results — coin flips, dice, passwords, the wheel — come from your browser's cryptographic random number generator.

Your browser keeps a few small things, and none of them leave your device: whether you picked the light or dark theme; what you last typed into each tool, only while the tab stays open, so going back doesn't wipe your work; and the stopwatch, timer and typing-test best scores, so a refresh doesn't lose them. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes them all.

Open source

The barcode generator uses JsBarcode and the QR code generator uses qrcode-generator, both MIT licensed. Passphrases in the password generator use the EFF long word list, under CC BY 3.0 US. The typeface is Archivo, under the SIL Open Font License.
