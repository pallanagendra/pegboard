About and privacy

What this site is, and what happens to what you type.

What this is

{{siteName}} is a collection of small, single-purpose web tools. Each one does a single job, opens fast, and gives you a result you can copy, download or print right away.

Your data stays on your device

Every tool runs entirely in your web browser. When you type text, paste a URL or generate a code, nothing is sent to a server. There are no accounts, no tracking scripts and no advertising cookies.

Your browser remembers two small things, and neither leaves your device: whether you picked the light or dark theme, and — only while the tab stays open — what you last typed into each tool, so going back doesn't wipe your work. Close the tab and those inputs are gone.

Open source

The barcode generator uses JsBarcode and the QR code generator uses qrcode-generator, both MIT licensed. The typeface is Archivo, under the SIL Open Font License.
