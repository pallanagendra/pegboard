About and privacy

What this site is, and what happens to what you type.

What this is

{{siteName}} is a collection of small, single-purpose web tools. Each one does a single job, opens fast, and gives you a result you can copy, download or print right away.

Your data stays on your device

Every tool runs entirely in your web browser. When you type text, open a photo, paste JSON or generate a password, nothing is sent to a server. There are no accounts, no tracking scripts and no advertising cookies. Random results — coin flips, dice, passwords, the wheel — come from your browser's cryptographic random number generator.

Your browser keeps a few small things, and none of them leave your device: whether you picked the light or dark theme; the last few tools you opened, to list them under “Recently used” in the menu; what you last typed into each tool, only while the tab stays open, so going back doesn't wipe your work; and the stopwatch, timer and typing-test best scores, so a refresh doesn't lose them. Photos and PDFs you add are only held in the page's memory while it's open. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes them all.

Open source

The barcode generator uses JsBarcode and the QR code generator uses qrcode-generator, both MIT licensed. HEIC photos are decoded with libheif (LGPL 3.0, built by libheif-js) and PDFs are merged with pdf-lib (MIT). Passphrases in the password generator use the EFF long word list, under CC BY 3.0 US. The typeface is Archivo, under the SIL Open Font License.
